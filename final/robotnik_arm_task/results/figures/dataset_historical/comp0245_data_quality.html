Decisions for training

VariantDecision
A: RawKeep as baseline; preserves instantaneous expert inputs.
B: StandardScalerFit only on training inputs, then reuse unchanged. Scaling does not remove derivative noise.
C: Causal filter + scalerCandidate for the 6 base derivative and 7 arm acceleration columns only. Fit scaler on filtered training values. Compare 5/10/20 ms using training evidence and later validation performance.

Initially leave positions, joint angles, measured joint velocities and target XYZ unsmoothed. Do not average wrapped yaw. Preserve the 33-input/11-output schema and output labels. Investigate extremes rather than clipping automatically. Filtering introduces lag and may remove information used by the expert; lower standard deviation is not proof of improved learning. Test distributions must not select preprocessing. Row statistics weight long trajectories more heavily; median per-trajectory standard deviations provide a complementary view. Initial zero derivatives and filter warm-up are included.
